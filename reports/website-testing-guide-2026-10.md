## 1. Before you start {#start}

| Tip | Why |
|---|---|
| Use a **private / incognito window** for every search test. | Your own past searches change what Google shows you. |
| Test on a **phone** as well as a computer, with location turned on. | Most patients search on phones, and local results depend on location. |
| Be patient with Google. | Changes to the website usually show in Google within **1 to 4 weeks**. Better positions build over **2 to 3 months**. AI assistants can take longer. |
| Write the result down each time (date, test, result). | So you can compare month by month. The tables below have space for this. |
| All tools named in this guide are **free**. Find each one by searching its name on Google. | No installation needed. |

## 4. Rich details check {#rich}

Behind each page, the website now gives Google a structured "fact card": the clinic's name, address, map location, opening hours and logo; Dr. Divya's qualifications; each treatment; and the questions and answers. This test confirms Google can read them.

### 4.1 Google Rich Results Test

Search Google for **Rich Results Test**, open Google's tool, paste the page's address, and press **Test URL**.

| Page to test | What the tool should list | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Home | **Local business** (dental clinic) and **FAQ**, with no errors | ☐ |
| Contact | **Local business** and **FAQ** | ☐ |
| Team | **FAQ** | ☐ |
| Root Canal Treatment (or any of the 10 detailed treatments) | **Breadcrumbs** and **FAQ** | ☐ |
| Tooth Replacement | **Breadcrumbs** and **FAQ** | ☐ |
| Services, Areas We Serve, How to Choose a Dentist | **Breadcrumbs** | ☐ |

*Warnings in yellow (for example "missing optional field: price range" or "missing image") are fine. Red errors are not; tell the developer.*

*Note: Google now shows question-and-answer drop-downs in results mainly for large health and government sites, so you may not see them in search. The questions still help Google and AI assistants understand and quote the clinic.*

## 7. Google Search Console checks (once access is given) {#gsc}

Search Console is Google's free dashboard for website owners. It needs Divya to give Prodman Nexus access (her action item 1). Once it is connected:

| # | Where in Search Console | What to do / check | What good looks like | Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Sitemaps** | Submit the site map (enter **sitemap.xml** in the box). | Status **Success**, **22** pages discovered. | ☐ |
| 2 | **URL inspection** (search bar at the top) | Paste the home page address, then **Test live URL**. | "URL is available to Google". Repeat for one treatment page and one new page. If a new page says "not on Google", press **Request indexing**. | ☐ |
| 3 | **Pages** | Look at "Indexed" and "Not indexed". | Indexed climbs towards 22 within a few weeks. Old-address pages may be listed as "Page with redirect" or "Alternate page"; that is normal. | ☐ |
| 4 | **Performance → Search results** | Set the date to the last 28 days. Look at Queries and Pages. | Searches with "chitlapakkam", "dr divya" and treatment names appear. Clicks and impressions rise month by month. | ☐ |
| 5 | **Core Web Vitals** | Mobile and Desktop reports. | Shows real visitors' speed. Expect "needs improvement" on mobile until the photos are fixed. | ☐ |
| 6 | **Enhancements** (Breadcrumbs, FAQ) | Check for errors. | Valid items, no errors. | ☐ |
| 7 | **Bing Webmaster Tools** (separate free tool) | Sign in and choose "Import from Google Search Console". | The site and site map appear in Bing too (Bing also feeds ChatGPT search). | ☐ |

## 10. Testing calendar and what to do if a test fails {#calendar}

| When | Tests |
|---|---|
| After every website update | Section 2 (quick check) |
| Every two weeks | Section 3 (Google searches) |
| Monthly, first week | Sections 4, 5, 6, 7, 8 and 9. Prodman Nexus includes the results in the monthly report. |
| 13 November 2026 at the latest | Domain renewal (Section 6, test 3) |

| If this fails | What it usually means | Who fixes it |
|---|---|---|
| A page does not open, or shows an error | The last update has a problem | Prodman Nexus (roll back to the previous version, then fix) |
| Page name in Google is different from the Appendix | Google has not refreshed yet (wait 2 to 4 weeks), or Google chose its own wording | Prodman Nexus checks in Search Console |
| Rich Results Test shows red errors | The fact card behind the page is broken | Prodman Nexus |
| Speed score low | Heavy photos or animations | Akash (designer) |
| Contact form does not deliver | Form not connected | Akash (designer) |
| AI assistant gives wrong details | An outside listing has old details | Prodman Nexus (listings) with Divya (Google Business Profile) |
| Padlock warning or domain warning | Certificate or domain renewal | Prodman Nexus, urgently |
