**Why these changes:** today none of the 20 pages has the location in its main heading, and only 1 page uses its main keyword in the text. Google reads the main heading and the first lines to decide what a page is about. Each page gets **one** main keyword, used naturally — no repetition, and no words such as "best", "painless" or "guaranteed" (dental advertising rules).
