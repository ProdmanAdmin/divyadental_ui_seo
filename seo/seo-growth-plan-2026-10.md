## 8. What We Will Never Publish {#rules}

Dentists in India follow the Dental Council's code of ethics. On **14 October 2026** the National Dental Commission advised all dentists against publishing patient testimonials and before/after photos, recording procedures, misleading claims and self-promotion. To protect Dr Divya's registration, we will **never** publish:

| We will never publish | Instead we use |
|---|---|
| Claims that the clinic is "best", "top" or "No. 1" | Helpful information — for example a guide on *how to choose a dentist* |
| Patient testimonials, patient photos or names, before/after photos | Educational graphics, the doctor's introduction, clinic photos |
| Videos or photos of procedures | Simple diagrams, animations and short talking tips |
| Guarantees such as "100% painless" or "permanent results" | "Gentle", "comfortable", "designed to minimise discomfort" |
| Offers or discounts in titles, listings or posts | Plain information about services and timings |

**Decisions we need from you:**

| Decision | Why |
|---|---|
| Keep or remove the 3 patient testimonials currently on the website | The advisory cautions against publishing testimonials |
| Keep or remove the Home-page offers (free consultation, "Upto 700", referral) | Offers may count as solicitation under the rules |
| Confirm your designation wording and experience (7.5 years practice; 7.8 years teaching) — and whether the "10+ years" counter on the website should change | Qualifications must be shown exactly and accurately |

*We could not read the original regulation text; the advisory was reported by one news source. Please confirm with the Tamil Nadu Dental Council or your IDA branch before any paid advertising. Full detail: Appendix D.2.*

#### D.2 Advertising rules — full table

Dentists in India are bound by the *Revised Dentists (Code of Ethics) Regulations, 2014* (Chapter VI, Clauses 8.1–8.3 cover advertising, patient solicitation, publicity and signage). On **14 October 2026** the Ethics and Dental Registration Board of the National Dental Commission issued an advisory to all registered dentists, as reported by Medical Dialogues. Breaches may lead to a show-cause notice, suspension or removal from the dentist register. Everything below is built to stay on the safe side.

| Topic | What the advisory cautions against | What we do in this plan | Decision for Divya |
|---|---|---|---|
| Patient testimonials & before/after photos | Publishing them | None in meta text, posts or ads. Use educational graphics, the doctor's introduction and clinic hygiene photos instead. | **Important:** the website currently shows 3 testimonials on Home and every treatment page. Please confirm whether to keep them (see note below). |
| "Best", "No.1", "top" about the clinic | Self-aggrandising publicity and misleading claims | "Best dentist in Chitlapakkam" is what *patients type*, so we target it with helpful pages (e.g. *How to choose a dentist*), but never call the clinic best. Titles and posts use "gentle", "family", "child-friendly". | Approve wording. |
| Recording / broadcasting consultations and procedures | Cautioned against | No procedure videos or reels. Use animations, diagrams and talking-head tips. | — |
| Offers and discounts ("free consultation", "Upto 700 off", referral rewards) | Not named in the advisory, but covered by the solicitation clauses | Offers are kept out of titles, descriptions and social posts until confirmed. | Check the offers on the Home page with the Tamil Nadu Dental Council / IDA. |
| Health claims ("100% painless", "permanent cure", "guaranteed") | Misleading treatment claims | Use "gentle", "comfortable", "designed to minimise discomfort". No guarantees, no results promises. | Approve wording. |
| Large signboards | Disproportionate signboards | Not part of the digital plan. | Keep clinic signage modest. |
| Qualifications, designations and experience | Not cautioned against when factual; misleading or inflated claims are | Show BDS, MDS, Associate Professor, Adult and Child Dental Surgeon, Consultant Paediatric Dentist exactly as Divya approves. State experience as two separate facts (7.5 years practice, 7.8 years teaching) — never add them into one bigger number. | Confirm the exact designation wording and whether the site's "10+ years" counter should change. |
